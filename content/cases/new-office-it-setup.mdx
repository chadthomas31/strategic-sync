An accounting firm signed a new office and needed it running before the team moved in. New computers in boxes, no email set up, no shared files, no backups.

We set up eight workstations, migrated everyone to Microsoft 365 with email on the company domain, configured the network printers, and put a Synology NAS in place for shared files and automated backups.

Staff showed up on move-in day, logged in, and got to work — no scrambling, no "the printer won't connect," no lost files.
