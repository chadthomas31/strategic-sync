A two-story home had strong WiFi by the router and nothing upstairs. Video calls dropped in the back bedroom, the smart TV buffered, and the backyard had no signal at all.

We surveyed the layout, picked the right number of eero units, and placed them for full coverage instead of guessing. Then we set up the app, a guest network, and parental controls.

Now every room — and the patio — has fast, reliable WiFi that hands off cleanly as you move through the house.
